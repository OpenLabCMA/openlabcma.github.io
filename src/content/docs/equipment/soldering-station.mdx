## Before you start

1. Fume extractor on whenever the iron is hot.
2. Iron back in the stand every time it leaves your hand. Turn it off when you leave the seat.

## Quick reference

- Tin the tip before and after use.
- Sponge/brass wool to clean, not files or sandpaper.
- Burns: cold running water, then tell someone. First aid kit at {/* EDIT: location */} the lab.
