## Before you start

1. Fast printers fail fast too; watch the first layer.

## Workflow

1. Slice in Bambu Studio with the printer's profile.
2. Send the job (LAN or SD card, per lab setup).
3. Watch the first layer. Remove prints only after the bed cools.
