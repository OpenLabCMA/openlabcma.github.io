Everyone who uses OpenLab equipment signs the user agreement once.

## Rules

1. Leave your station clean; scraps in the bins, tools back on the board.
2. Report breakage and near-misses; nobody gets in trouble for reporting.
3. No food or drink at the machines.
4. Sign the OpenLab Access Agreement before using Equipment.
