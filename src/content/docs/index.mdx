A low-friction prototyping, teaching, and community space in the CMA Lab at HKUST(GZ). Open 24/7.

Maker equipment with a focus on textiles and w-textiles: machine embroidery, sewing, serging, coverstitch, 3D printing, robotics, electronics, and wide-format photo printing. Faculty teach classes here; PhD and MA students use the equipment for research; MFA students use OpenLab as home-studio space.
